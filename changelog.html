v7.3.0
Jul 2026
Push to Nuvio & PWA Foundations Latest
Direct account linking to push catalogs, collections and avatars straight into Nuvio — no more manual copy/paste. Plus PWA groundwork for the upcoming Android app.
🚀Push Directly to Nuvio — Link your Nuvio account right from Step 4 and push catalogs, collections, or both straight to a selected profile. Existing addons and collections are always merged, never wiped.
👤Profile Picker with Real Avatars — Multiple Nuvio profiles now show as a visual picker with actual profile avatars. Single-profile accounts skip the picker entirely.
📱Scan-to-Push QR — Generate a branded QR code on the Finalize page and push your setup straight from your phone. Optional "Remember me" keeps you signed in on that device for future scans.
🎭Avatar Vault Direct Assign — Link your Nuvio account on the Avatar Vault page and tap any of the 226 avatars to set it directly on a profile, no more copy/paste into Nuvio.
🛡️Clearer Error Messages — Nuvio push failures now show exactly what went wrong (network issue, wrong password, or a Nuvio-side error) instead of a generic failure message.
📲PWA Foundations — Full icon set (regular + maskable), install screenshots, service worker refinements and manifest improvements laid down ahead of the Android app release.
