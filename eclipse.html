How to find your ARL

1

Log in to deezer.com in your browser.

2

Open DevTools with F12 and go to the Application tab (Chrome) or Storage tab (Firefox).

3

Navigate to Cookies → deezer.com and find the cookie named arl.

4

Copy the full value — it's a long string of letters and numbers. Paste it above.
